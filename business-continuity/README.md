# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

## Objective

Connect cybersecurity and technology resilience with the organization's ability to continue delivering critical services during disruption.

## Core Concepts

- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Critical process identification
- Dependencies
- RTO and RPO
- Business Continuity Plans (BCP)
- Disaster Recovery Plans (DRP)
- Crisis coordination
- Testing and exercises
- Lessons learned and improvement

## Resilience Lifecycle

**Understand → Prioritize → Plan → Prepare → Test → Recover → Improve**

## DRP Testing

A recovery plan should be validated through exercises rather than treated as a document that is assumed to work.

A structured test should define:

- Scope
- Scenario
- Participants and responsibilities
- Preconditions
- Recovery sequence
- Expected RTO/RPO
- Evidence to collect
- Success criteria
- Issues and lessons learned
- Corrective actions and owners

## Business Perspective

The technical question is not only:

> Can the server or platform be restored?

The business question is:

> Can the organization restore the critical service within the level of disruption it has agreed it can tolerate?

This distinction connects infrastructure recovery with business continuity and executive risk management.
